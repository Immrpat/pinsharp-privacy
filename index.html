Privacy Policy

Pinsharp (app.pinsharp.enhancer)
Publisher: Immrpat
Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how Immrpat (“we”) handles information in the Android app Pinsharp. It applies only to this app. Pinsharp sharpens, unblurs, and upscales photos on the phone, and can optionally rebuild faces in a photo. It has no account system, no advertising, and no network permission, so it cannot upload your photos to us or to anyone else.

Photos stay on your phone

You choose a photo with Android’s photo picker, or you share a photo into Pinsharp from another app. The app reads that photo so it can show it and enhance it. Enhancement, including face restoration, runs on the device with models stored inside the app. The app does not upload the photo, a face crop, or the result.

The enhanced image stays in the app’s memory until you save it or leave. If you tap Save, Pinsharp writes a new file to Pictures/Pinsharp in your gallery through Android’s media library. That copy is yours. Other apps can reach it according to your device settings. Pinsharp does not send the saved file anywhere.

For a JPEG you save, the app copies the original photo’s capture date into the new file so your gallery can keep the original day. It does not copy GPS, location, camera identity, or other metadata into the saved file. The original photo in your gallery is left as it was.

The app does not collect an account, your name, your contacts, your location, an advertising identifier, analytics events, or crash reports for a server. It does not include an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not sell photos or any other information.

Permissions

Pinsharp asks Android only for what the enhancement needs on the device:

  • Photos you pick or share in. The photo picker and Android’s share sheet give the app access to the image you choose. The app does not request broad storage access and does not scan your gallery.
  • Notifications. On Android 13 and later, the app may ask to post a notification so a long enhancement can show progress and tell you when it is finished. The notification stays on your device.
  • Foreground service. A long enhancement is allowed to keep running while you switch apps. This does not send the photo off the device.

The app does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or the advertising ID. The on-device inference library declares a network permission; Pinsharp removes that permission, so the library cannot contact a server either.

What we do not receive

We do not operate a server for Pinsharp. We do not receive your photos, faces, saved files, or notification contents. The machine-learning libraries in the app run locally. We do not share photos with the authors of those libraries.

Android backup

Pinsharp does not keep a photo library in its private storage. An enhanced photo that you have not saved exists only in memory and is discarded when the job is gone. A photo you saved lives in Pictures/Pinsharp in your gallery, not as an app-private copy we control. Android’s own device backup applies its rules to the app’s private data. That private data is not your photo library.

How long information stays

Uninstalling Pinsharp removes the app. It does not delete photos already saved to Pictures/Pinsharp. Delete those with your gallery or file tools.

We do not keep your photos on a server, so there is no server copy to delete. Email pat23.w@gmail.com if you have a privacy question.

Children

Pinsharp is not directed at children under 13. It does not offer accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children on a server.

Security

Photos are processed on the device, and the app has no network permission. No method of on-device storage is completely secure. The app is designed to limit collection by keeping enhancement local and by not operating an account, advertising, or photo-upload service.

Changes

We may update this page when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. The same address is linked inside the app.
